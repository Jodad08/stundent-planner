Prerequisites, units and requirements come from the SF State Bulletin 2026–27. The planner treats “or permission of the instructor” as not available, assumes in-progress courses pass, and fills open seats with GE or free-elective units until you reach 120.

The bulletin doesn't say which semesters a course runs. Every course is assumed to run each fall and spring unless you set its offering in the course details.
