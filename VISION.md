# VISION: GatorGraph

**Thesis.** Same SFSU degree requirements, three planners: SFSU's official roadmap, a deterministic prerequisite engine, and Gemini constrained by that engine. Watch which plans break prerequisite rules, which reach graduation soonest, and which fit the student's career goal, with every rule traced to a Bulletin quote.

**User.** An SFSU Computer Science student planning their semesters (P0: a fresh 8-semester plan).

**Pain (observed in SFSU's Student Center, `prompt.md` A.4).** SFSU's planner lists what to take. It does not show how courses connect, why a course is placed where it is, or how a plan fits a career goal. Prerequisites appear nowhere in the degree audit.

**Demo moment.** Drag CSC 340 before its prerequisite: the line goes bold, the semester turns red, and the engine names the rule. Then "AI Plan → machine learning": Gemini proposes, the engine rejects or repairs, and the final plan is valid.

**Non-goals.** Login, SFSU system integration, other majors, transfer/AP credit, section times, mobile.
